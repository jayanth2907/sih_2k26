## 1. Canonical Status Taxonomy

Throughout this audit, every capability, data flow, and interface is classified under one of seven mutually exclusive technical status labels:

| Status Label | Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`IMPLEMENTED`** | Fully built, end-to-end verified across backend, frontend, and tests with live runtime mechanics and complete data models. |
| **`IMPLEMENTED_SIMULATED`** | Architectural pipelines, calculation engines, and workflows are fully implemented, but live runtime inputs are driven by deterministic physics/operational simulation engines rather than direct industrial SCADA/hardware connections. |
| **`SOURCE_DERIVED`** | Derived directly from authentic statutory government publications (e.g., Coal Controller's Organisation Summary of Coal Blocks 2024, DGMS safety circulars, Ministry of Coal guidelines). |
| **`SYNTHETIC_DEMO`** | Seeded deterministic mock data structured according to statutory schemas for evaluation, demonstration, and automated test fixtures. |
| **`EXTERNAL_ADAPTER`** | Architectural gateway/adapter interface with resilience patterns (circuit breakers, timeouts, cached fallbacks) built for external statutory systems (CMSMS, PARIVESH, DGMS). Operates in simulation mode in the absence of production government API credentials. |
| **`HUMAN_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`** | System captures, analyzes, or predicts operational signals, but requires explicit authorized human supervisory sign-off or physical verification before legal, regulatory, or operational finality. |
| **`NOT_IMPLEMENTED`** | Scope item not implemented or reserved for future hardware/statutory deployment phases. |

## 2. PS Requirement-by-Requirement Coverage Matrix (A through Q)

### Category A: Real-Time Monitoring
* **Problem Statement Requirement:** Continuous tracking of underground and opencast environmental parameters, atmospheric gasses, equipment telemetry, and operational statuses.
* **Backend Implementation:** FastAPI streaming services (`app/api/v1/sensors.py`, `app/api/v1/websocket.py`, `app/services/sensor_service.py`), physics-based gas dispersion engine, sliding window aggregations, anomaly detection.
* **Web Implementation:** 3D Digital Twin with volumetric methane/CO heatmaps, live telemetry HUDs, sensor drill-down modals (`DigitalTwinPage.tsx`, `SensorsPage.tsx`).
* **Mobile Implementation:** `MobileHomeScreen.tsx`, `MobileFieldReportingScreen.tsx`, atmospheric thresholds display, real-time warning indicators, `NetworkStatusBadge.tsx`.
* **Evidence & Test Suites:** `backend/tests/test_phase2_telemetry_anomalies.py`, `backend/tests/test_sensors_cameras.py`.
* **Demo Scenario:** Scenario 1 (Ventilation Stoppage & Gas Spike) and Scenario 3 (Critical Inundation Alert).
* **Data Source:** Deterministic physics telemetry simulator reflecting CMR 2017 operating thresholds.
* **Status:** `IMPLEMENTED_SIMULATED`
* **Known Limitations:** In demo mode, telemetry data is generated by an internal numerical simulation loop rather than direct RS-485 / Modbus PLC physical sensor feeds.

### Category B: AI & Predictive Analytics
* **Problem Statement Requirement:** Predictive risk modeling, anomaly detection, early warning indicators for hazard escalation, and explainable AI insights.
* **Backend Implementation:** `trinetra-risk-escalation-v1.0-histgbm` pure-NumPy gradient boosted decision model (`app/ml/models.py`, `app/services/predictive_service.py`), directional signal attributions, 30-minute escalation horizon.
* **Web Implementation:** Predictive Risk Intelligence center, ROC/PR curve visualization, calibration curves, directional signal charts (`PredictiveRiskPage.tsx`, `AnalyticsPage.tsx`).
* **Mobile Implementation:** `MobileRiskIntelligenceScreen.tsx`, `MobileHomeScreen.tsx` risk card, field verification flow with outcome capture and downstream task linking.
* **Evidence & Test Suites:** `backend/tests/test_phase5_predictive_risk.py`, `backend/tests/test_mobile_risk_intelligence.py`.
* **Demo Scenario:** Scenario 2 (Predictive Risk Escalation on BDS-04 Level 3).
* **Data Source:** Simulated operational dataset with strict chronological train/val/holdout splits (70/15/15) and zero future leakage.
* **Status:** `IMPLEMENTED_SIMULATED` / `HUMAN_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`
* **Known Limitations:** The model outputs calibrated probability and directional factor rankings; predictions are early warnings and **do not** constitute confirmed regulatory violations until field-verified by an authorized officer.

### Category C: Geo-Tagged & Time-Stamped Field Activity
* **Problem Statement Requirement:** Geo-spatial tagging and immutable time-stamping of inspections, hazards, evidence, and incident reports.
* **Backend Implementation:** `FieldInspection`, `FieldEvidence`, `AuditEvent` data models (`app/models/field_operation.py`, `app/models/audit.py`), SHA-256 fingerprint generation, WGS84 GPS coordinate validation, bounding-box checks.
* **Web Implementation:** Interactive Leaflet GIS Command Map (`GisMapPage.tsx`), spatial markers, coordinate inspections, layer filtering.

* **Evidence & Test Suites:** `backend/tests/test_mobile_evidence_gps.py`, `backend/tests/test_phase12b_gis.py`.
* **Demo Scenario:** Field officer conducts safety audit at BDS-04 Seam 4, captures photo evidence, records device location coordinates.
* **Data Source:** Browser/Device Geolocation API + Server-side SHA-256 hashing.
* **Status:** `IMPLEMENTED`
* **Known Limitations:** GPS coordinates represent browser/device-reported location metadata; underground spatial positioning relies on mapped level/zone references as satellite GPS signals attenuate underground.

### Category D: Offline Capability & Idempotent Synchronization
* **Problem Statement Requirement:** Uninterrupted field operation in disconnected underground colliery environments with reliable synchronization upon reconnection.
* **Backend Implementation:** Idempotent batch sync endpoint (`/api/v1/mobile/sync`), client-generated UUID deduplication, conflict resolution rules, `FieldSyncLog` auditing (`app/services/field_service.py`).
* **Web & Mobile Implementation:** `frontend/src/mobile/screens/MobileSyncCenterScreen.tsx`, IndexedDB/localStorage offline mutation queue, automatic network reconnect listeners, conflict triage UI.
* **Evidence & Test Suites:** `backend/tests/test_mobile_sync_center.py`, `backend/tests/test_mobile_field_inspection_flow.py`.
* **Demo Scenario:** Inspector toggles offline mode, conducts inspection, creates observations, reconnects to Wi-Fi, and executes conflict-free batch push.
* **Data Source:** Local client queue synchronized to PostgreSQL/SQLite backend.
* **Status:** `IMPLEMENTED`
* **Known Limitations:** Browser storage is subject to client-side localStorage/IndexedDB storage quotas (typically 5MB - 50MB); large video evidence files are queued with binary chunk safeguards.
